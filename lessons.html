QUICK OVERVIEWS

Academic Reading & Writing

Read a passage carefully, identify its main idea, and look for supporting details. When writing, plan your point first, support it with relevant examples, and revise for clarity.

Scientific Thinking

Scientific thinking starts with observation and a focused question. A hypothesis is a possible explanation that can be tested. Record evidence honestly and explain what the evidence supports.

Business Basics

Businesses provide goods or services to meet customer needs. Basic planning considers the customer, resources, costs, price, and how value is delivered.

Communication & Society

Effective communication considers the audience, purpose, and context. Listen actively, use clear language, and respect different perspectives.

Digital Literacy

Use strong unique passwords, keep devices updated, check sources before sharing information, and avoid posting personal details publicly.

Study Skills

Set a realistic weekly goal, study in short focused sessions, practice retrieving information from memory, and review difficult topics again later.
